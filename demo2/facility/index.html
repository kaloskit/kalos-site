The Studio

A private floor, built for one thing.

Kalos is appointment-only. You'll never wait for a rack, share a coach's attention, or train for an audience — the floor holds five clients at most, each with a coach of their own.

Rendering — the Kalos floor

01 — The Space

What makes it different.

Most gyms are built to hold as many people as possible. Ours is built the other way. Every station is set up before you arrive and reset before you leave — you walk in, warm up, and do nothing but the work. We handle everything else.

02 — Equipment & Rooms

Everything the method needs. Nothing it doesn't.

The floor is equipped for serious physique work — free weights, racks, and the machines the programming actually calls for, chosen by the head coach for the method rather than for show. Off the floor, a lounge gives you somewhere to land before and after your session: Kalos is built to be a studio you genuinely enjoy spending time in, not just a room you train in.
