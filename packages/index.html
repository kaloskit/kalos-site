KALOS Pro Access.

Every package includes KALOS Pro. Your custom training and nutrition programs live right in the app, with daily and weekly progress logs that turn into visual charts you can follow over time. Snap a photo of your meal and our AI estimates the calories automatically, or ask the built-in AI assistant to help you log on the go. Everything feeds straight into your progress dashboard.

On its own, KALOS Pro is $15.99 per month. It is included at no extra cost for as long as you have an active package. Your first step is free: a 60 minute consultation, in person.
