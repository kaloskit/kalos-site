Welcome to Kalos

Why Kalos exists

Kalos is a 1-on-1 personal training studio built around a single niche: physique transformation. We don't compete on convenience, group classes, or the cheapest rate. We compete on results — real, visible, measurable transformations that clients can see, feel, and prove.

Most of the personal training industry sells time. A client books a session, shows up, and hopes something improves. At Kalos, we sell something different: a complete, structured journey toward a specific goal — with a proven system, a dedicated trainer, and a technology platform that documents every step. The session is not the product. The transformation is the product.

Our Mission

Kalos is built to transform physiques and lives. Through personalized 1-on-1 coaching, a proven goal-driven system, and technology that makes progress visible, we help clients achieve the results they came for — in a community built on trust, support, and genuine care.

How we think about the client journey

Every client starts with a real conversation, not a sales pitch. We help them set a goal that is specific, measurable, relevant, achievable, and time-bound — a clear destination, not a vague hope of "getting fit." From there, the trainer builds the strategy: the training plan, the nutrition approach, and the pace of progress that fits that specific person.

As the client trains, everything is tracked in the Kalos app — lifting performance, physique changes, progress photos, and body weight — and presented back to the client in a way that's visual and easy to understand. This isn't administrative box-checking. It's how clients stay motivated, how trainers make smarter adjustments, and how Kalos grows.

A note on trainer judgment

Living these values does not mean following a rigid script. Values 2 and 4 in particular exist to protect your ability to make the right call for the client in front of you — which exercises to test, how heavy to load a set, when to push and when to pull back. That judgment is yours, and it's one of the most valuable things you bring to this job.

What isn't optional is the system underneath that judgment: a clear goal, a documented plan, and accurate tracking of everything that happens. Trainer autonomy lives inside the Kalos system — not outside of it. That's how every client gets a great trainer and a great result, no matter who's coaching them.

Our Methodology, In Brief

This is a summary. The full Coaching Methodology manual covers session protocol, in-session judgment, and non-negotiable standards in detail — read that document for the complete picture. This section exists to connect the methodology to the mission and values above.

The training cycle

Every Kalos program runs on a training cycle, typically 4 weeks (ranging 3–5 depending on the client's goals and needs). The cycle always begins with a Strength week to test and measure performance, and always ends by feeding that data back into the next cycle.

WeekPhaseFocusIntensity
1StrengthTest performance, set baseline (1RM)75–80% 1RM · heavier, lower reps
2Hypertrophy 1Build muscle, moderate volumeHeavier, lower volume
3Hypertrophy 2Build muscle, more volumeLighter, higher volume
4Hypertrophy VolumeMaximum volume, break plateausIntensity techniques, most volume

Strength week: setting the baseline

In Strength week, the trainer selects which exercises to test — this choice is flexible and based on the client's program — but the methodology never changes. The client works in a lower rep range at heavier loads, and on select exercises performs an AMRAP test (As Many Reps As Possible). The result is entered into the 1RM calculator, generating an estimated one-rep max. This number becomes the foundation for every phase that follows: the trainer uses it to calculate the right working weights for higher-rep hypertrophy work, without guesswork.

Hypertrophy 1, Hypertrophy 2 & Hypertrophy Volume

These three phases build muscle through progressively increasing training volume — starting heavier and lower-volume in Hypertrophy 1, moving lighter and higher-volume in Hypertrophy 2, and reaching maximum volume in Hypertrophy Volume, where intensity techniques like supersets, drop sets, and failure sets may be used to break plateaus. All working weights in these phases are calculated as a percentage of the 1RM established in Strength week.

The cycle repeats — progressive overload, the Kalos way

At the end of the cycle, the client returns to a Strength week and retests. If performance has improved, working weights increase accordingly for the next cycle. If a client's performance dips for any reason — life stress, poor sleep, injury — the program adjusts down to meet them appropriately. This is how Kalos achieves real progressive overload: not by assuming clients always move forward, but by measuring where they actually are, every single cycle.

Why this matters for the business

Every AMRAP test, every logged set, and every progress photo is more than a training record — it's proof the system works. This data is what keeps clients motivated, what lets trainers make sharper adjustments, and what becomes the honest, results-based marketing that brings in the next client. Track accurately, and you're not just coaching your client — you're building Kalos.

What We Expect From You

These are the minimum standards for every Kalos trainer, on every session, with every client — the practical expression of our values and methodology. They apply regardless of how you choose to program or coach an individual session.

StandardWhat it means
Pre-session prepEvery session is planned in advance — exercises, weights, and rep targets — before the client walks in.
ProfessionalismEquipment is set up before the client arrives and reset before they leave. Their time and experience are respected at every step.
Cycle adherenceSessions follow the correct phase (Strength / Hypertrophy 1 / Hypertrophy 2 / Hypertrophy Volume) for where the client is in their cycle.
Active coachingEvery rep is counted and watched. Trainers are present and engaged — not distracted — for the full session.
Real-time judgmentWeight and intensity are adjusted between sets based on what you observe, using your professional judgment.
Accurate trackingEvery set, weight, and rep is logged before the session ends. 1RM is updated whenever retested.
Client encouragementClients are encouraged to track their own journey alongside you — it's part of what keeps them motivated and invested.
