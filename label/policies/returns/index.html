Returns

All Sales Are Final

Every piece is made to order for you, so we don't take returns or exchanges for size or a change of mind. Check the size guide on each product page before you order.

Something Wrong With Your Order

If your item arrives with a defect, a misprint or damage, or it isn't what you ordered, we'll replace it or refund you at no cost. Reply to your order confirmation email within 14 days of delivery or pickup with your order number and a photo of the issue. Please don't send anything back unless we ask you to.

Cancellations

You can cancel within 24 hours of placing your order for a full refund. After that your piece is in production, so the order can't be cancelled.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Questions

Reply to your order confirmation email and we'll get back to you.
