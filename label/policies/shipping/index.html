Shipping

Made to Order

Every piece is made for your order after you check out. Production takes 1 to 2 weeks, then your order ships.

Where We Ship

We ship to Canada and the United States, and shipping is free on every order. We don't ship to other countries yet.

Delivery Times

Once your order ships, delivery usually takes 2 to 7 business days in Canada and 5 to 10 business days to the US. The carrier sets these times, so treat them as estimates.

Order Updates

We email you when your order is processed, with an estimated delivery window. You'll get a second email with your tracking number when it ships.

Pickup

You can choose free pickup at the KALOS studio at checkout. We email you with pickup details when your order is ready, and we hold it for 30 days.

Duties and Taxes for US Orders

Orders ship from Canada. The carrier may charge US import duties or taxes on delivery. Customs sets these charges, and the buyer pays them.

Address Changes

Please check your shipping address at checkout. If something is wrong, reply to your order confirmation email within 24 hours and we'll fix it before your order goes into production. Once a parcel ships, we can't redirect it.

Lost or Damaged Parcels

If tracking shows your parcel as delivered but you can't find it, tell us within 7 days and we'll help you open a claim with the carrier. If your order arrives damaged, see our Returns policy.
