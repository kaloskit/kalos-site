The Team

One standard. Every coach.

Your result at KALOS doesn't depend on which coach you get. Every trainer here is trained and certified to the same documented method before they ever run a session solo, so what you're really choosing is the system, delivered by a professional who fits you.

Co-Founder · Head Coach

Matt Law

Matt Law is a Natural IFBB Pro and the overall Men's Bodybuilding winner at the 2025 CPA Xtreme Natural Muscle Pro Qualifier (pro card earned, October 25, 2025), with decades of personal training experience and a documented track record of client transformations. He created the KALOS method (the training cycle, the standards, and the coaching playbook every KALOS trainer certifies against) and leads the training floor.

He's coached everyone from first-timers to competitive athletes, and his own competitive career is the proof of concept: the same system KALOS clients train on is the one that built it.
