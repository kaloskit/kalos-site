PRICING

How Much Does Personal Training Cost at KALOS?

One-on-one sessions run $100 to $130 CAD per session depending on package size: Starter is 12 sessions at $130 ($1,560), Momentum is 24 at $115 ($2,760), Transformation is 36 at $105 ($3,780), and VIP is 48 at $100 ($4,800). Every package gets the same coaching standard and full tracking in the KALOS app; larger packages simply cost less per session.

THE DIFFERENCE

How Is KALOS Different From a Regular Gym?

KALOS isn't a membership gym. The floor is private and appointment-only: no crowds, no waiting for equipment, and every station set up for you before you arrive. Coaching is strictly one-on-one, and the studio is built for one outcome — physique transformation, with the numbers to prove it.

COACHING

Does It Matter Which Coach I Get?

No — and that's the point. Every KALOS coach runs the same proven method, so your result doesn't depend on trainer roulette. You're matched to the coach who fits your goal, and the standard is identical across the team.

Meet the team

NUTRITION

Is Nutrition Coaching Included?

Yes. Your program covers training, nutrition, and cardio as one plan. You log food and hit macro targets in the KALOS app, and your coach reviews the data with you — the same numbers driving your training drive your nutrition.

TRACKING

How Is Progress Measured?

Every session, lift, and check-in is logged in the KALOS app: personal records, body-weight trends, food, and cardio. Strength is anchored by max-effort benchmark sets, and your muscle-building volume is calculated from your own numbers. Progress reviews happen off the floor, in a proper sit-down.

WHO IT'S FOR

Do I Need Training Experience?

No. The method starts from your numbers, wherever they are — first-timers and experienced lifters run the same system at different loads. What you need is a goal. The consultation turns "get in shape" into a specific, measurable target with a date on it.
