About KALOS

The classical ideal of the body, pursued through modern measurement.

KALOS, Greek for beautiful or excellent, names the classical ideal of the complete physical form. We pursue that ideal the modern way: measured, engineered, individual.

01 · Mission

Why KALOS exists.

Most of the personal training industry sells time: a client books a session, shows up, and hopes something improves. KALOS sells a complete, structured journey toward a specific goal, backed by a proven system, a dedicated coach, and a platform that documents every step. The product isn't the session. It's the transformation.

KALOS is built to transform physiques and lives. Personalized 1-on-1 coaching and technology that makes progress visible get clients the results they came for, inside a community built on trust and genuine care.

02 · Core Principles

Seven principles behind every decision.

From how we program a single session to how we grow the studio, the same seven principles decide it.

01

The journey, not the session

We're judged by the transformation, not the hour. Every session is one step in a bigger plan.

02

Follow the system

Set a clear, time-bound goal. Build the plan. Execute it. Trust the process that's already worked.

03

Track everything, guess nothing

If it's not documented, it didn't happen. The lifts, the weight, the photos, every time.

04

Adjust based on data

Numbers don't lie. Tracked progress drives the plan, not gut feel alone.

05

Results are the marketing

We don't oversell. The transformations speak for themselves.

06

Attract the right clients

Honest results bring in people who want exactly what we offer. They're easier to coach and get better outcomes.

07

Support each other

Win or lose, we lift each other up. Trust and encouragement come before ego.

03 · The Founders

Art and science.

KALOS was founded by two people who couldn't be more different, and that's the point: Matt Law, a Natural IFBB Pro with decades of coaching and a documented record of client transformations, and Kit Wan, a software engineer who believes a great method deserves a system that can carry it. One built the method, the other encoded it, and what you train on is both.

We're not a commercial gym, a class studio, or a wellness lounge. KALOS is a private studio with one focus, run on one standard.

KALOS Personal Training Studio (Kalos PT Studio Ltd.) is an independent personal training studio at 12060 Vulcan Way in Richmond, BC. KALOS Personal Training in Richmond, BC is not affiliated with Kalos Health, Inc.
