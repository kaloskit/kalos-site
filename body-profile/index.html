01 — What It Is

A finish line you can frame.

Body profiles started in Korea, where they became the way everyday people mark a transformation. You put a shoot date on the calendar, train seriously for three or four months, and on the day a photographer captures the result. Most of the people who do one are not athletes. They wanted a clear goal, a deadline, and proof.

We took the format and made it ours. There is no white studio and no stage tan. Your profile is shot at Kalos, with the equipment you trained on, by a photographer we bring in for the day. Your coach plans the block so you arrive at the shoot at your best, and the app holds every set and weigh-in behind the photos.

02 — How It Works

Four steps to Profile Day.

The shoot is the last two hours of a twelve-to-sixteen-week block. Everything before it is the training you would be doing anyway, pointed at a date.

STEP 1

Lock the Date

Add the Body Profile to your package and your coach books your slot on a Profile Day 12 to 16 weeks out. It goes straight into your app calendar. That date is the point.

STEP 2

Build

Train your program as normal. Check-in photos every two weeks, weigh-ins and lifts tracked in the app, and nutrition tightened by your coach as the date gets closer.

STEP 3

Polish and Pose

The final four sessions add posing practice and a two-week polish plan. No water cuts and no crash diet. You should look like yourself on the day, at your best.

STEP 4

Profile Day

A 90-minute shoot on the studio floor with our photographer and your coach. Three set-ups under professional lighting, and a private gallery within ten days.

04 — Not a Bodybuilding Show

The show without the stage.

A bodybuilding show asks you to diet to the bone, stand under lights next to strangers, and be scored. A body profile keeps the part that works, the date and the discipline, and drops the rest. There are no judges and no weight class. The only comparison is you on day one.

That also means no extreme cut. Your coach sets a conditioning target that fits your timeline and your life, and the final two weeks are a polish, not a crash. The profile is meant to be a physique you can keep, photographed at its best.

WHAT WE KEEP

The date and the discipline.

A day on the calendar. A block of training with a point to it. Posing practice, so you know how to present what you built.

WHAT WE DROP

Everything that only lasts an afternoon.

Judges and placings. Stage tans and water cuts. A physique you can only hold for one day.

06 — Questions

What people ask before they book.

Do I need to be lean already?

No. The block is designed to get you there. Most profiles start from an ordinary starting point. The photos show what changed.

What if I'm not ready by the date?

Your coach checks in at week eight. If you both agree you need more runway, your profile moves to the next Profile Day, once, at no charge.

Who sees the photos?

You. Every photo is private, in the same locked storage as your check-in photos. Nothing goes on the wall or online without your written consent.

What do I wear?

Whatever you would train hard in. Shorts, a sports bra or a fitted top, nothing you would not wear on the floor. You will settle the look with your coach in the posing sessions.

Can I do one without a package?

The profile is the end of a training block, so it comes with a Transformation or VIP package. Active clients with at least twelve weeks of sessions ahead can add one too.

Is there a crash diet?

No. No water manipulation and no starvation week. Your coach sets a target that fits your timeline, and the last two weeks are a polish. You keep the result.
