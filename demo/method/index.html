The Method

Progression isn't hoped for. It's engineered.

Every Kalos program runs the same proven system — created by our head coach, an IFBB professional, over decades of competition and client transformations. Our own software computes it, so you never wonder what to lift or whether it's working.

01 — The Training Cycle

Four phases. One engine.

Your program runs in cycles of roughly four weeks. Each cycle opens by measuring your strength and ends by feeding that data into the next one — so the weights you lift are always calculated from what your body can actually do, never guessed.

WEEK 1 · STRENGTH

Test & set the baseline

Heavier loads, lower reps. On key lifts you'll finish with a max-effort set — the number that anchors everything ahead.

WEEK 2 · HYPERTROPHY I

Build, heavier

Muscle-building work at moderate volume — heavier weights, controlled reps, calculated from week one's numbers.

WEEK 3 · HYPERTROPHY II

Build, higher volume

Lighter and higher-volume — more sets, more reps, the stimulus shifting so your body never fully adapts.

WEEK 4 · VOLUME

Peak & break plateaus

Maximum volume with intensity techniques — supersets, drop sets, failure sets — to force the adaptation home.

Then the cycle repeats: you retest, and if you've gotten stronger, every working weight moves up with you. If life got in the way — stress, sleep, an off week — the program adjusts down to meet you. That's real progressive overload: measured, every single cycle, not assumed.

02 — The Anchor

Every weight, calculated from your max.

Your one-rep max — measured safely through a rep-out test, never a risky single — is the anchor for every prescription in your program. Each phase trains at a precise percentage of it. When your max moves, everything recalculates. It's the mechanism that makes progression objective and eliminates guesswork — and the platform runs the math automatically, every session.

03 — The App

The method, in your pocket.

Every client trains with the Kalos app — custom training and nutrition programs delivered right in the app, with daily and weekly progress logs that turn into visual charts you can track over time. Snap a photo of your meal and our AI estimates the calories automatically, or ask the built-in AI assistant to help you log on the go. Everything feeds straight into your progress dashboard.
