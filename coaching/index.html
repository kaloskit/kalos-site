01 — How It Works

Coaching, minus the commute.

Online coaching at Kalos isn't a PDF and a monthly email. It's the system our studio clients train on every day, run by a coach who reviews your week, every week, and adjusts the plan while it's happening.

02 — The Loop

Train. Track. Check in. Adjust.

STEP 1

Your program, in the app

Your coach writes your training block around your goal, your schedule, and whatever gym you have. Every session is laid out: exercises, targets, progressions.

STEP 2

Log as you lift

Record every set in the app. PRs, estimated 1RMs, and training history build automatically, the same records our in-studio clients compete on.

STEP 3

Nutrition without the spreadsheet

Snap a photo of your meal and the app estimates the macros. Weight, steps, and cardio sync from your watch. Your coach sees the full picture, not a guess.

STEP 4

A real weekly check-in

Once a week your coach reviews your numbers, your photos, and your notes, then answers with adjustments and the plan for the week ahead. Questions in between go straight to chat.

03 — What You Get

The studio system, in your pocket.

PROGRAMMING

A plan built for your gym

Not a template. Your coach programs around the equipment you actually have and updates the block as you progress through each phase.

ACCOUNTABILITY

A coach watching the data

Every workout, weigh-in, and meal you log lands in front of your coach. Slipping for a week isn't invisible, and neither is a PR.

COMMUNITY

The Kalos leaderboard

Online clients train on the same records and consistency boards as studio clients. You're remote, not alone.

NUTRITION

Targets, not lectures

Weekly macro targets set by your coach, meal-scan logging that takes seconds, and a nutrition review in every check-in.

PROGRESS

Proof it's working

Progress photos, strength trends, and body-weight curves in one place, reviewed with your coach block by block.

ACCESS

A message away

Direct chat with your coach for form questions, substitutions, and the day the hotel gym has one dumbbell.
