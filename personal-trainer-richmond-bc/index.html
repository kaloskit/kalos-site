Common questions

Personal training in Richmond, answered.

How much does a personal trainer cost at KALOS in Richmond, BC?

One-on-one personal training at KALOS costs $100 to $130 CAD per session, plus GST, depending on package size: Starter is 12 sessions at $130, Momentum is 24 at $115, Transformation is 36 at $105, and VIP is 48 at $100. KALOS Pro app access ($15.99 per month on its own) is included with every active package.

Is the consultation free?

Your first step is a free 60 minute consultation, in person, covering your goal, your training history, and the plan to get you there. You buy nothing before that conversation, and there is no obligation after it.

Where is KALOS, and what are the hours?

KALOS is at 12060 Vulcan Way, Richmond, BC V6V 1J8, Canada. Once the studio opens on November 1, 2026, it is open every day from 9 AM to 9 PM, by appointment. Phone: (778) 949-9944.
